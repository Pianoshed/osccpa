About Us

Who We Are

The Ondo State Consumer Protection Agency is dedicated to protecting consumer rights and promoting fair, transparent market practices. We address complaints, enforce compliance, and strengthen public confidence in the marketplace.

Enforcement

Robust market surveillance and compliance monitoring across all sectors.

Advocacy

Public education and consumer rights awareness campaigns statewide.

Mediation

Fair dispute resolution between consumers and businesses.

Policy

Evidence-based regulations for sustainable market development.
